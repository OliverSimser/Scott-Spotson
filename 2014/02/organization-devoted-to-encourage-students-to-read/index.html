The Litpick website is a terrific website designed to encourage students in primary and high school grades to read books that are family friendly, yet compelling reads. "Seeking Dr. Magic" was recently reviewed and given five stars. Read the review below, and do visit the website!
http://www.litpick.com/review/seeking-dr-magic-review-bkim
Bkim
Age at time of review - 13
“Seeking Dr. Magic” is about a former F.B.I. agent who was forced to retire and became a private detective after being shot in the arm. His name is Tony Hetfild, and he became famous for solving a crime involving a murdered celebrity. Eventually Tony finds himself pitted against the infamous Dr. Magic. Dr. Magic is known as an all-powerful sorcerer who can do anything he wants with magic except bring people back from the dead and read minds. Dr. Magic sends his minions, the Phantom Ninjas and the Rainbow Seekers, to make stunts on the streets and create chaos. Tony has to do something dangerous that involves risking death in a tortuous way. He has to defeat the dangerous Dr. Magic. Tony has to use Dr. Magic’s two weaknesses against him, his identity and his past. Defeating Dr. Magic could lead to Tony’s own demise.

Opinion:

“Seeking Dr. Magic” is a phenomenal book. After the first few pages, you’ll be hooked. This is a must-read book that you can’t possibly put down! This is a book that the whole family will enjoy. It has mystery, action, adventure, magic of course, and yes, even a little romance. This book deserves six stars, but I give it five because that is as high as you can go. Your satisfaction is guaranteed!
