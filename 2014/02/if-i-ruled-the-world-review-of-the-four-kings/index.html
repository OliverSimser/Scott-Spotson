"If I Ruled the World"-- Review of "The Four Kings"

http://www.amazon.com/review/R2155B36CI3HQ7 5.0 out of 5 stars By C. M. Lance Amazon Verified Purchase(What's this?) This review is from: The Four Kings (Kindle Edition) If the world was completely messed up politically and economically (are we that far away?), and my friends and I had enormously powerful magic-how would we fix it? The author has put a lot of thought into that unique premise. Numerous political/economic theories are laid out and reviewed for fit. I'm surprised "trickle down" wasn't given play. The challenges involved in making a massive change over to a "fairer" system are explored. this book is educational as well as entertaining. The character development is good, as it is revealed that wizards come from all walks of life, and their personas are shaped by their nurture environment. The writing makes it a solid four, but because of the uniqueness, the way it made me think, and the educational aspects, I'm giving it a five.
Scott Spotson
