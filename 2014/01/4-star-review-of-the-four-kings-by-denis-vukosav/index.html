4-Star Review of “The Four Kings” by Denis Vukosav

From the original website · 2014-01-20

http://www.amazon.com/review/R3FX7DS5LXDM80

4.0 out of 5 stars Original and different By Denis Vukosav This review is from: The Four Kings (Paperback)

“The Four Kings” by Scott Spotson is novel that brings an intriguing concept about some modern world that is lead by wizards.

Therefore if you are searching for some interesting title this one could be recommended because it combines some magic motives in same time introducing some economical and political ideas which make up this story seems almost realistic.

Original and different…
