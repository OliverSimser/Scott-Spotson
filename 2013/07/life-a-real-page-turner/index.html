Life II - A real page turner

From the original website · 2013-07-05

Not your regular time travel book - If I could go back I would re-write the first sentence to say "I enjoyed this novel so much, I can see myself reading it a second time".

Follow Max as he goes back to his 16-year-old self and tries to forge his destiny into a new one called Life II. This book is over 600 pages but is a fast read.

The writing is exciting, riveting, and thrilling. It's a real page turner, and you will be waiting with baited breath as you wonder what is going to happen next.

The author explores a lot of philosophical issues involved in time travel that I found interesting. Some were are not resolved which left me wanting more! "Life II" packs a punch, and I think most people would enjoy this story.
Scott Spotson
