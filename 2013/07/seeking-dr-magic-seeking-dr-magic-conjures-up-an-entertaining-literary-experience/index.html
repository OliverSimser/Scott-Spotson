SEEKING DR. MAGIC - Seeking Dr. Magic conjures up an entertaining literary experience!

I have not read a fantasy book in over 2 years, but after reading Dr. Magic, I have come to question why I have overlooked the genre for such a long time. In this tale of magic and mystery, Spotston delivers action and intrigue, which drew me further into the story with every page. I wanted to space out my time reading the Kindle version of this book, but the allure of the classic hero versus villain motif drove me to marathon the experience all the way to the end. I can certainly identify with Hetfield, but I think that everyone wishes at one point or another in their lives that they had magical powers, so I can also say that I can identify with Dr. Magic. The cat-and-mouse game that they play throughout the story reinforces this notion; I would root for Hetfield in one chapter, only to root for Dr. Magic in another. All in all, the experience of reading Seeking Dr. Magic was an excellent reintroduction to the fantasy genre.
Scott Spotson
