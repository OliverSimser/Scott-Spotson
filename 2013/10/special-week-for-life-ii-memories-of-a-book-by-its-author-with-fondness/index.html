Special Week for "Life II"! Memories of a Book by its Author - With Fondness

Life II was written in June, July, and August 2012. It was published February 5, 2013.

The first chapter of Life II sees Max Thorning start a normal day - or so he thought - on October 22, 2013. He finds a mysterious book titled, "Account of Time Travel on Earth Using Wave Theory."

He spends the week trying to figure out clues to this mysterious book.

On October 27, 2013, the last day he lives his original life, he encounters a portal that takes him back to September 16, 1987, as his sixteen-year-old self, but with the memories he had as of October 27, 2013.

It is with a bit of happiness, and sadness, that I mark the dates I used for Life II.  It's been a great journey, which will continue into infinity as more readers discover this book.



This was the original cover for Life II below.  While I found it very relevant, a book cover needs a single message, and overarching direction. But it's fun to see Max as he ages from age 16 to 42!  And at the end of the book, we learn that he's been taking a photo of himself every year during Life II.  So this ties in.
