Hey all, here is a piece I wrote for the terrific website Eat, Sleep, Write concerning my book "Delusional" which is unusual in that the comprehensive plot was developed by a man (myself), but refined and expanded with layers of depth, expanding it to nearly double the size, by a woman.  Here I discuss what I had learned from following a female author's progress through my manuscript!
