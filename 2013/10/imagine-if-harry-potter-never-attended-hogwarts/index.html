Imagine if Harry Potter Never Attended Hogwarts

From the original website · 2013-10-18

But instead left home upon finding out he was a wizard (leaving behind the overbearing Dursleys) at age 10, when the series started, instead of being "found" by the wizards and accepted into Hogwarts? Moreover, what if Harry Potter was the ONLY wizard in the world? When you read "Seeking Dr. Magic," you can imagine that premise, although Dr. Magic was written as its own story. Seeking Dr. Magic is available as a recommended bargain book on this popular website: http://ereadernewstoday.com/more-ent-deals-for-10-17-13/6735342/ #harrypotter #wizard #fantasy
