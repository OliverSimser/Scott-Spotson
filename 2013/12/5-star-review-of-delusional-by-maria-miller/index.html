5-Star Review of “Delusional” by Maria Miller

http://www.amazon.com/review/R2HPEGMPFM40XQ

5.0 out of 5 stars Great Read By Maria Miller, Author Format:Kindle Edition|Amazon Verified Purchase

DELUSIONAL weaves a psychic thriller, a crime thriller, and a romance together to make an incredible tale. The author cleverly interlinks a twisted plot with a seemingly benign romance that will keep the reader on the edge of his seat. This novel kept me turning the pages to see exactly who was who as the plot unfolded.
Scott Spotson
