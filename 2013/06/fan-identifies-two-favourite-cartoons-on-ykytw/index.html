Fan Identifies Two Favourite Cartoons on YKYTW...

From the original website · 2013-06-06

http://alanabookreview.blogspot.ca/2013/06/you-know-youre-thin-when-by-scott.html I got this book in a Goodreads Giveaway. "You Know You're Thin When..." is a short book of quick, funny joking book with slings about being skinny. I like how the first and last joke were the same, gives it a kind of closure. "You Know You're Thin When..." reminds me of a day to day calendar of "You Know You're a Redneck When..."My favorite was "You know you're skinny when... you don't feel sorry for the horse when you ride on it." My mother cracked up as well. My second favorite was "You know you're skinny when...you don't suck in your belly when your photo is being taken." Haha... I didn't like how there was randomly two of the same in a row, but the pictures that came with each joke was good and added more humor.
