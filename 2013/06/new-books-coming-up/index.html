New Books Coming Up...

From the original website · 2013-06-16

"Delusional" is on its way, and should be published by August.

See the excerpt:

Patricia Fowler has it all: she’s an attractive 25-year-old single woman, university educated, with her own spacious condo, a best female friend, and a dream job as marketing researcher. However, she does something that she’d always vowed she’d never do: she falls in love with Paul, a colleague at work who’s not only married, but also a manager. As the two plot their secret relationship, Patricia suddenly experiences a series of hallucinations. She believes that her dress has suddenly reversed its color pattern just as she’s wearing it. She perceives that her purse is on fire on the light rail on her way to work, and she throws it out of the window, causing the train to screech to a stop. And there’s more. Devastated, she fears she is developing a severe mental illness. What she doesn’t know is that the images are real – and someone – or something – is purposely causing the delusions that are disrupting her life, her career, and her relationship with Paul. When she finds out what is causing her virtual nightmares, she has to find a way to stop her tormentor – before it’s too late.

Another book I am currently starting to write is "The Four Kings."  It's too early to reveal much, but basically it will involve a group of wizards with extraordinary powers called the Liberators taking over the governing of the world (as we know it), and how Mortals (humans) adapt and resist, in turn.
