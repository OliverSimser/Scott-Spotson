Website will go live on Apr.15th

From the original website · 2013-04-12

The ScottSpotson.com website is set to go live April 15! I hope you will find the information on the website useful and exciting to read! It is personally very exciting for me to write three books, all within months of each other, and watch step by step as they go through interior design, cover design, and finally posting on the Internet! Needless to say, the most exciting part however is writing. If you enjoyed reading my books just as much as I enjoyed writing them, I consider that a wonderful synergy!

As a movie director would say, "let's roll!"
