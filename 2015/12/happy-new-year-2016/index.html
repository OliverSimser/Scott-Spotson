HAPPY NEW YEAR 2016!

Brits sure like me!

I've had four contracts with Brits who are excited to be authors.  Two were in non-fiction, one dealing with athletics, and the other dealing with getting great grades at university.

For the other two books with Brits, both fiction, one book dealt with a blokes' holiday, with raunchy humour, and the other book focused on three women dealing with the ups and downs of modern urbane life.

The other clients I have are Americans. I've written a girl's series, and it's loads of fun. One book I'm co-authoring with another American is an epic fantasy and deals with a brave young woman seeking to right the wrongs of her father, an evil king.



 

 

 
Scott Spotson
